# Third-party content

The Apache-2.0 licence in `LICENSE` covers Sheltr's source code only. The document text that Sheltr bundles and quotes belongs to its publishers and keeps its own licence. The per-document licence and attribution are recorded in `corpus/manifest.yaml` and carried into every bundle. The apps show them to users.

Text from these documents appears in `apps/mobile/android-sample-bundle/` and in published bundles:

| Document | Publisher | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| "Prepare" (https://prepare.campaign.gov.uk/) | UK Government | [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/). Contains public sector information licensed under the OGL v3.0. |
| "Public awareness and public education for disaster risk reduction: key messages (Short version)", 2013 | International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC) | Copies of all or part of the guide may be made for noncommercial use, providing the source is acknowledged. Requests for commercial reproduction go to the IFRC (secretariat@ifrc.org). |

## If you fork or redistribute

- Keep the attribution with the text.
- The IFRC text is **non-commercial only**. A fork that distributes it commercially needs IFRC permission, or must remove it from the corpus and sample bundle.
- The manifest records an IFRC regional mirror URL. The canonical ifrc.org copy has not been verified.

Sheltr is an independent project. It is not an official government or agency service.
